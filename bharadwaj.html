The Experience

An Evening
Unlike Any Other

Bharadwaj's Autograph is more than a concert — it is an immersive musical journey. As night falls over Tenkasi, the air transforms with melody, rhythm, and artistry in a setting that is both intimate and extraordinary.

"Every note is a signature. Every moment, a memory."

Held at the enchanting Vel's Vidhyalaya CBSE School grounds, the evening promises a carefully crafted live music experience — where the music speaks directly to the heart. Gates open at 5:00 PM, with the concert beginning at 5:30 PM and continuing through midnight.
